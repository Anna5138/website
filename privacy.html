Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 29, 2026

Dental Arts of Great Valley ("the Practice," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information in connection with our website at dentalartsofgv.com and our internal automated SMS notification service. This Policy does not cover the protected health information ("PHI") we collect in the course of providing dental care to our patients, which is governed by our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices, available at the office upon request.

Information We Collect From Website Visitors

When you visit our website, we may collect:

  • Information you voluntarily provide through contact or appointment-request forms, such as your name, email address, telephone number, and any message content you choose to send us.
  • Standard technical information automatically logged by web servers, such as your browser type, device type, IP address, and pages visited. This information is used to maintain and improve the website.

How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Respond to inquiries, schedule appointments, and communicate with prospective and current patients.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our website.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell information collected through our website. We do not share contact information with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS / Text Messaging Service

Internal Automated Alerting

The Practice operates an internal automated alerting system that sends SMS messages exclusively to the Practice's own office telephone line. These alerts are received and acted upon by Practice staff in the course of their employment.

Program Description. When the Practice's automated assistant ("Judy") completes an insurance benefit verification, an SMS alert is sent to the Practice's office line so that staff may review the verification in the Practice's clinical workflow systems.

Recipients. Messages are sent only to the Practice's own office telephone line (610-540-7008), and are received by Practice staff. No SMS messages are sent to patients, consumers, or third parties via this service.

Consent and Opt-In. Because messages are sent only to the Practice's own line and are received by Practice staff as part of their employment, consent is established through the Practice's internal employment relationship. There is no public opt-in form because no public recipient is messaged.

Frequency. Low volume — typically 1 to 5 messages per business day, dependent on insurance verification workload.

Cost. Standard message and data rates may apply, as charged by the recipient's mobile carrier. The Practice does not charge any fee for receiving these messages.

Opt-Out. Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Reply HELP for help, or contact the Practice directly at 610-540-7008.

Third-Party Disclosure. Messaging consent and mobile information are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

HIPAA and Patient Health Information

Information you provide to us as a patient — including health history, treatment records, and insurance details — is protected health information ("PHI") under the federal Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act ("HIPAA"). Our handling of PHI is described in our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices, which is provided to every patient at their first visit and is available at the office on request.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the information we collect from loss, misuse, and unauthorized access. No method of transmission over the Internet, however, is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Our website is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 through our website. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.
