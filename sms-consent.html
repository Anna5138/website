SMS Consent & Opt-In

Effective Date: June 4, 2026

This page describes the consent and opt-in process for the internal automated SMS alerting service operated by Dental Arts of Great Valley ("the Practice"). It is provided as the consent record for our toll-free messaging service.

Program Overview

The Practice operates an automated internal alerting service that sends SMS text messages to the Practice's own office telephone line (610-540-7008). When the Practice's automated assistant completes an insurance benefit verification, an alert is sent to the office line so that Practice staff may review the result in the Practice's internal workflow systems. Messages are sent only to the Practice's own office line and are received by Practice staff. No messages are sent to patients, consumers, or any third parties.

How Consent Is Obtained

Because this service messages only the Practice's own office line, the authorized representative of the Practice (the owner) provides consent on behalf of the business for that line to receive these messages. Consent is captured verbally using the script below, which is read to and affirmatively agreed to by the authorized representative before the line is enrolled.

Verbal Consent Script

"Dental Arts of Great Valley will send automated text message alerts to this office line at 610-540-7008 to notify staff when an internal task, such as an insurance benefit verification, is complete. Message frequency varies, typically one to five messages per business day. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe, or reply HELP for assistance. Our Privacy Policy is available at dentalartsofgv.com and our Terms of Service describe this program. Do you agree to receive these text messages on this line?"

The line is enrolled in the SMS service only after the authorized representative answers affirmatively ("Yes") to the question above. A record of this consent is retained by the Practice.

Program Details

Internal Automated Alerting Service

Recipients. Messages are sent only to the Practice's own office telephone line (610-540-7008). No SMS messages are sent to patients, consumers, or third parties.

Message Frequency. Low volume — typically one to five messages per business day, dependent on insurance verification workload.

Cost. Message and data rates may apply, as charged by the recipient's mobile carrier. The Practice does not charge any fee for this service.

Opt-Out. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from these messages. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact the Practice directly at 610-540-7008.

Sample Message. "Dental Arts of Great Valley: A new insurance benefit breakdown is ready for review in the app and email. Please verify. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."

Privacy. Mobile information and messaging consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. This service is used exclusively for internal operational notifications and is never used for marketing.
